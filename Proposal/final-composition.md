# Team Information 1 (max 200 words)
(Tell us a bit about your team: How did the team come together? Is this the first team outing or are you returning to SC (Alternatively: Newcomers v. Experienced Team Members)?)

Our team consists of students from two Universities located in the Helsinki metropolitan area.
The team was assembled by the Finnish supercomputing center: CSC, and most of the training is provided through them.

Finnish teams have competed in IndySCC in 2022 and 2023, and this year we are excited to compete on-site for the first time.  
All of this years team members are first-timers in IndySCC/SCC.


# Team Information 2 (max 200 words)
(How is the team preparing: Are there any events / sessions / meetings you are running? Have you worked with hardware or application partners?) 

We are being trained by advisors from the Finnish Supercomputing center: CSC. 
The team meets with the advisors every few weeks on average and trains on the different competition applications and benchmarks. 

We will meet before the competition with developers at CSC that have been working with NAMD for a long time in the LUMI system. 
This should give us detailed information on how to run the application in the most efficient way possible on the cluster.

We also are in contact with people from AMD on how we can get the most performance from the MLPerf benchmark suite (and the other benchmarks) on our hardware.

Thus far, we have been training with the Finnish supercomputers, including the LUMI supercomputer. The hardware in LUMI closely resembles our competition cluster and provides a good testing environment early on in the training.  
We work in close collaboration with our hardware provider, HPE, who will provide us with training on how to assemble our competition cluster and how we can use it in the most efficient manner. 

# Team Information 3  (max 200 words)
(Is there anything interesting or special about this team you would like to share with us?)

We are the first Finnish team competing on-site at SCC and excited to represent both University of Helsinki and Aalto University.  
Our team name: Team Löyly, represents the hiss and steam generated from water thrown onto sauna rocks. In our opinion, this embodies the Finnish culture and draws a great parallel to the (sometimes) scorching hot servers we will be competing with.
